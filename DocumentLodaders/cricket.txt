The Song of the Willow

The sun beats down upon the green,
A hushed suspense, a vibrant scene.
The polished orb of cherry red,
A gentle nod from the umpire's head.

A rhythmic run, a gathering pace,
The bowler bounds to reach the crease.
A sudden leap, the arm swings high,
The seam cuts sharp across the sky.

The batsman waits with focused eye,
To let it pass or let it fly.
A subtle shift, the willow swings,
A sweet, crisp crack as leather rings.

It races fast across the grass,
Where fielders dive to let none pass.
A sudden throw, the flashing bails,
The crowd erupts, a loud exhale.

It’s more than runs upon a board,
Or centuries that heroes scored.
From turning spin to fiery pace,
It is a game of skill and grace.
